Step 1: Put Mallet into Bootloader (DFU) Mode

1. Firmware updates using Chrome / Edge / Bluefly (iOS Ipad / IPhone) only.
2. Wake the mallet, wait for the blue flashing LED.
3. Plug in the USB-C cable into the mallet and your computer.
4. Click below to connect via Bluetooth and enter DFU mode.

4. The mallet will reboot into DFU mode.

Step 2: Install Firmware

Leave the USB-C cable connected to the mallet and your computer, then click Flash Firmware and select the XIAO nRF52840 serial port. Do not unplug until complete.
